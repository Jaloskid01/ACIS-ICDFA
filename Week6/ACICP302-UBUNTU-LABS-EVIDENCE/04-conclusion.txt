ACICP302 Activity 04 — Evidence-Based Conclusion
Date: 14 October 2026
VM: icdfa-VMware-Virtual-Platform

Source inspection confirmed explicit loopback configuration for the
plant Modbus/TCP service (127.0.0.1:5020), direct dashboard
(127.0.0.1:8080), guard endpoint (127.0.0.1:5021), and guarded
dashboard (127.0.0.1:8081).

The recorded read-only TCP inventory showed listeners on 127.0.0.1:5020
and 127.0.0.1:8081. No listeners appeared on ports 8080 or 5021 at
that time. Local DNS and printing listeners were also present.

The observations support the intended loopback scope for the application
listeners that were active. They do not prove that every route is secure,
that guard rules are complete, or that services will always bind safely.
The risk register therefore separates observed evidence from potential
consequences that were not tested.

Activity scope was limited to source inspection and read-only local
inventory. No external targets or attack scripts were used, and no
process values or services were changed.
