ACICP302 — Activity 04 Risk Register
Date: 14 October 2026
Scope: Approved student lab source and local loopback only

R1 — Modbus/TCP process-data exposure
Evidence: plant.py lines 26-27 configure 127.0.0.1:5020.
Inventory: 127.0.0.1:5020 was listening.
Potential consequence: Unauthorized access to process telemetry;
write capability could affect simulated process values if permitted.
Status: Exposure beyond loopback was not observed or tested.
Preventative controls: Keep the service loopback-bound; restrict clients
and write operations according to the approved lab design.
Detective controls: Review application logs and monitor unexpected
Modbus write requests.

R2 — Direct dashboard availability
Evidence: dashboard.py line 224 binds the dashboard to 127.0.0.1:8080.
Inventory: Port 8080 was not listening during the recorded check.
Potential consequence: If enabled without adequate access controls,
an exposed dashboard could disclose process information or permit
unauthorized interaction through available routes.
Status: Not listening at inventory time; route permissions not assessed.
Preventative controls: Loopback binding, authentication where appropriate,
and least privilege.
Detective controls: Review dashboard access and application logs.

R3 — Guard service and guarded dashboard
Evidence: guard.py lines 19-22 configure the plant target at
127.0.0.1:5020 and guard endpoint at 127.0.0.1:5021.
dashboard_guarded.py lines 8-9 target 127.0.0.1:5021;
line 265 binds the dashboard to 127.0.0.1:8081.
Inventory: Port 8081 was listening; port 5021 was not listening.
Potential consequence: If guard rules are incomplete or bypassable,
unauthorized requests might not be adequately controlled.
Status: Guard-rule completeness and enforcement were not tested.
Preventative controls: Validate authorization and safety checks for
all relevant operations; retain loopback binding.
Detective controls: Review event logs and record rejected requests.

R4 — Service-binding drift
Evidence: The four application configurations specify loopback addresses.
Inventory: Observed application listeners on 5020 and 8081 were loopback-
bound. Ports 8080 and 5021 were not listening at the recorded time.
Potential consequence: A future broad bind could expose lab services
outside the intended boundary.
Status: No non-loopback application listener among the observed services.
Preventative controls: Keep explicit loopback bindings and isolate the VM.
Detective controls: Recheck listener addresses and VM network settings.

Conclusion:
The source configuration reviewed is loopback-bound. At the recorded
inventory time, the plant service on 5020 and guarded dashboard on 8081
were listening; 8080 and 5021 were not. This is a time-specific snapshot.
No external scanning, attack execution, process-value changes, or
unauthorized service modifications were performed for this activity.
