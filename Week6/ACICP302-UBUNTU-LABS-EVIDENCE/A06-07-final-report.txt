7. Log and evidence limitations
The displayed guard log contained entries timestamped 18:28 and
17:59 from earlier scenarios. No new Slow Drift-specific log entry
was confirmed. Repeated log rows were not treated as unique attack
counts. Terminal results and dashboard readings were recorded in
text files; screenshots still need to be captured and saved.

8. Conclusion
In the Attack Hold and False Data Injection tests, the direct path
reported unsafe process values, while the guarded-path scripts
reported values within configured limits and containment. In the
Slow Drift test, actual pressure readings stayed at or below the
40-bar limit on both paths, with a lower maximum reported on the
guarded path. The observations support the usefulness of rule-based
limits in these specific simulations; they do not prove protection
against every attack.

9. Safety and cleanup
Tests were conducted against the authorized local training
simulation. Do not run the DoS scenario without explicit instructor
approval. Stop lab processes cleanly, confirm the relevant lab ports
are no longer listening, preserve evidence, and restore the assigned
snapshot if instructed.
